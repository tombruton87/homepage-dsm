## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-10

First release. Homepage in Container Manager, filled in by the package:
- DSM with optional live stats; the password stays out of the YAML;
- the apps from this family, with their icons;
- other containers with a web page, each with up/down and response time.

Discovery runs every 10 minutes. A DSM window adds:
- Dashboard: hide tiles, add links of your own in groups of your own;
- Look: title, theme, colour, search box and clock;
- Edit by hand: a switch to take over the YAML in File Station;
- Site address: the allowed host names follow it.

Also included: backups, updates and removal.
